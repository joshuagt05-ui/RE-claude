# Book Plan: DOWNSTREAM

> One family, one year, and the end of the world as it reached a farm on the South Platte.

## The shape of the book
- **Five parts across one year**, from the night of the movie to its first anniversary.
- **Around 60 family chapters** in close third person with rotating POVs: Nora (about 40%), June, Ray, Walt, and Eli now and then.
- **12 Dispatches**, one per month. Each is a short standalone chapter set somewhere else in the world with a new narrator.
- **Every chapter opens with a map**, including the Dispatches (see `maps/README.md`).
- **Target length: about 330,000 words, or roughly 1,100 pages.** Family chapters run about 5,000 words and Dispatches about 3,000.

| Part | Title | Days | Months | Chapters | Dispatches | ~Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | **Boil Order** | 0–31 | Sep–Oct | 13 | I, II | 71k |
| II | **The Line** | 31–91 | Nov–Dec | 12 | III, IV, V | 69k |
| III | **The Quiet** | 92–182 | Jan–Mar | 13 | VI, VII, VIII | 74k |
| IV | **The Crossing** | 183–305 | Apr–Jul | 14 | IX, X, XI | 79k |
| V | **Salt** | 306–365 | Aug–Sep | 8 | XII | 43k |

**Tone:** Zach Cregger-style dread and gut punches, held in check by a family that's warm, funny and loves each other. The world ends, but the book isn't nihilistic. It earns a small, hard hope. **(open: see Decisions below.)**

## PART ONE: BOIL ORDER
*Day 0–31 · Sept 25 – Oct 26 · The world breaks: truth, then medicine*

The outbreak arrives slowly, then all at once. The Mercers watch Raccoon City on TV as something happening *somewhere else* while the river past their house has already been carrying it for a week.

**Key beats**
- **Ch 1, "Homecoming" (Day 0):** Friday night lights in Merritt, the same night as the movie. We meet the family. After the game, Ray finds a steer at the stock tank that **won't stop drinking.** Walt hears Raccoon City police traffic cut out mid-sentence. *(Map 1 has already been drawn.)*
- **The flash (Day 1):** news of "an Umbrella chemical explosion." Nora watches Boulder's skyline smoke on a livestream and her future quietly ends.
- **Carmen's last call from DIA (Day 3):** "Bring me back a pretzel," says Eli.
- **The cattle (Day 4–5):** the herd goes wrong. **The first bite in the book is an animal's**, a steer turning on Ray's hired hand.
- **Patient One (Day 6):** June's ER shift. A trucker from Denver can't stop drinking, and Dr. Rao gets bitten. **June comes home and says nothing** for a whole chapter.
- **Boil order (Day 7):** the town learns that the water they've been drinking for a week is the problem. **Who drank?** The six days of waiting for the Thirst are the horror center of Part One.
- **The packing plant (Day 8–9):** Fort Morgan. Mateo's family.
- **The Platte Line (Day 10):** the Guard closes I-76 and Merritt is fenced in with the sick.
- **They go to the water:** Eli notices the Turned walking toward the river, and his map in the chapter shows it.
- **End of Part One (Day 31):** New Orleans falls on the last good TV broadcast. The Mississippi is gone. Walt's map: pins going quiet.

**Dispatches:** **I · Departures** (DIA, Day 3) · **II · The Rhine** (Rotterdam, Day ~25)

## PART TWO: THE LINE
*Day 31–91 · Nov – Christmas · The world breaks: law, then supply*

Merritt learns to live behind the cordon. It's a siege story inside a small town: rationing, boiling, the Pruitt well, church-basement water lines, a sheriff holding things together, and neighbors turning on each other one bucket at a time.

**Key beats**
- The town organizes: boiling stations, rain barrels, the church as water depot. **Everything that burns becomes precious.**
- **Carmen's calls from Europe** as the Rhine falls and she heads north. Her last clear call is from a train in Denmark.
- **The Arklay Files leak (~Day 60):** the town learns Umbrella knew, and that **a cure existed for one night.** Rage with nowhere to go.
- **Thanksgiving (Day 62):** the best and worst dinner of Nora's life.
- **Rolling blackouts.** Walt quietly shows June the math on the grid. Insulin math starts.
- Mateo moves into the Mercer house.
- **Christmas (Day 91):** the grid flickers during dinner. It comes back, but not for long.

**Dispatches:** **III · Arklay Springs** (Umbrella, London) · **IV · Lagos** · **V · The Alpine Redoubt**

## PART THREE: THE QUIET
*Day 92–182 · Jan – Mar · The world breaks: power, communication, government*

Winter on the plains. The grid dies, the phones die and the Turned freeze in the fields. People mistake the stillness for peace. Then comes the Thaw.

**Key beats**
- **The Quiet:** frozen Turned standing in the snow-covered fields like fence posts. The kids dare each other to touch one.
- **The grid goes dark (~Day 105).** Walt's radio runs on a battery bank and one solar panel, and it becomes the whole world.
- **The Kessler brothers seize the Pruitt well.** The town fractures.
- **Insulin runs out (~Day 135).** **Nora's raid on the Sterling hospital pharmacy** is the big set piece of Part Three.
- **The Pacific Authority broadcast (~Day 150):** *"Clean water on the coast."* A destination appears.
- **(open) Walt.**
- **The Thaw (~Day 165):** the frozen wake up and the ice lets go. **The worst night of the book.**
- **Ray's chapter: leaving the farm (Day ~178).** He opens the gates for the animals that are left.

**Dispatches:** **VI · Tokyo** · **VII · The Nile** (Cairo) · **VIII · Salt** (the desalination states)

## PART FOUR: THE CROSSING
*Day 183–305 · Apr – Jul · The road west*

A road story across the American West: Wyoming, the Continental Divide, Salt Lake, the Nevada desert, the Sierra. Every chapter's map moves. Every place they pass is its own small world-building showcase for how a region survives or doesn't.

**Key beats**
- The Wyoming line militia at the state line.
- **Laramie: meeting Michelle** (April, about 7 months pregnant) and the voice memo.
- **The Continental Divide:** fresh snowmelt, the first clean water in months that nobody has to boil. Eli cries.
- **The Great Salt Lake:** a safe beach where you can't drink the water.
- **Bats (late June).**
- **The birth** on US-50 in the Nevada desert.
- **Lake Tahoe:** a paradise that's too good to be true.
- **Donner Pass in summer.**

**Dispatches:** **IX · The Flotilla** (Mediterranean) · **X · Reykjavík** (Carmen) · **XI · Mexico City**

---

## PART FIVE: SALT
*Day 306–365 · Aug – Sep · The coast*

The Pacific Authority is real, and it isn't paradise. The last part is about what it costs to get in, who gets left outside, and what a family is once it's finally safe.

**Key beats**
- Sacramento, and the refugee camps outside the gates.
- The test strips, the gate and the price.
- **(open)** Does Carmen make it back?
- **Day 365:** the ocean. Eli's last map.

**Dispatch:** **XII · Raccoon City, one year later.** The crater, the street and the vial. What is still in the hospital, and who has come looking for it.
