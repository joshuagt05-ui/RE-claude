# Global Chronology: Day −4 to Day 365

**Day 0 = Friday, September 25, 2026**, the night of the movie.
**Day 365 = Saturday, September 25, 2027.**

This is the world's timeline. The family's timeline sits inside it (see `book-plan.md`). The Mercers learn about each event late, only partly, or not at all. The reader, through the maps and Dispatches, often knows more than they do.

**Legend:** 🇺🇸 United States · 🌍 rest of the world · ☂ Umbrella · 🏠 the Mercers

---
