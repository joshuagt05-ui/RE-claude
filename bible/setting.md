# Setting and Geography

Real geography with a few fictional places added. Coordinates are in `maps/specs/`.

## Fictional places
| Place | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Raccoon City** | Arklay Mountains, west of Denver (≈ 39.84°N, 105.50°W) | The film's city. Umbrella's lab is under Raccoon City General. Destroyed on Day 1 at 05:52. |
| **Arklay Mountains** | A range in Colorado's Front Range | Taken from the games. |
| **Arklay River** | Runs from Raccoon City east to the South Platte, joining it north of downtown Denver | It has taken the place of Clear Creek on our map. This is where it all starts. |
| **Arklay Reservoir / Arklay Springs plant** | Just below Raccoon City on the Arklay | Umbrella's bottled-water plant. Source of Lot 0922. |
| **Merritt, CO** | On the South Platte and I-76, between Brush and Sterling (≈ 40.43°N, 103.45°W) | Home, population 2,311. It has a grain elevator, a Catholic parish, a K-12 school (the Merritt Mustangs), a Sinclair station and a bar called the Headgate. |
| **The Mercer farm** | Just east of Merritt, with river frontage | 900 acres, shallow irrigation wells (contaminated) and two stock ponds. |
| **The Pruitt place** | On the tableland south of the river | Has the deep Ogallala well. |

## Real places that matter
- **The South Platte:** a sandy, braided plains river, low in autumn and heavily used for irrigation. The towns along it (Brighton, Fort Morgan, Brush, Sterling, Julesburg) are strung along I-76.
- **Aurora's riverbank wells near Brighton:** the way the Denver metro drinks the Arklay. Denver's own mountain water stays clean, and it doesn't matter.
- **Fort Morgan meatpacking plant** (fictionalized): infected cattle arrive there, and it's one of the first outbreak sites.
- **Sterling:** county seat and June's hospital.
- **Denver International Airport:** the way the virus leaves Colorado for the world.
- **Ogallala Aquifer:** a deep, ancient aquifer under the High Plains. It stays clean.

## The route west (Part IV, proposed)
Roughly 1,400 miles from March to August, by truck, then on foot, then by whatever works.

| Leg | Route | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merritt → Cheyenne, WY | Back roads north to avoid I-76 and Denver. A militia checkpoint at the state line. |
| 2 | Cheyenne → Laramie | I-80 over Sherman Summit. The Laramie university holdouts. **Michelle.** |
| 3 | The Continental Divide | Great Divide Basin and Rawlins. **Snowmelt above any contamination: the first truly clean water in months.** A moment of joy. |
| 4 | Rock Springs → Evanston | The Green River. Where every river is presumed unsafe. |
| 5 | Salt Lake City | **The Great Salt Lake is brine, so the virus dies there.** A strange, safe beach where the water can't be drunk. The storehouse community. |
| 6 | US-50 across Nevada | "The Loneliest Road in America." Basin and Range desert in summer, where water comes from rain and springs. **Michelle's baby may be born here.** |
| 7 | The Sierra Nevada | The Lake Tahoe holdouts, then Donner Pass (in summer, with the Donner Party in the back of everyone's mind). |
| 8 | Sacramento → Monterey Bay | The Pacific Authority, its gates and the ocean. |

**(open)** Other endings to consider: the Gulf Coast (closer, grimmer), the Pacific Northwest, or staying and holding the farm with the Pruitt well. My vote is the Pacific. The ocean as the final image gives the book its shape.
