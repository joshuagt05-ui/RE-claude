# DOWNSTREAM

*A Resident Evil story: a fan continuation of Zach Cregger's* Resident Evil *(2026).*

> **The missile was on time. The river was faster.**

On the night Raccoon City falls, 130 miles downstream, the Mercer family is at a high school football game. Four days earlier, Umbrella's lab began leaking into the Arklay River. The river runs into the South Platte, the South Platte runs past their farm, and from there the water runs to everywhere else.

The book follows one family through one year, from the night of the movie to its first anniversary, as the world breaks down one system at a time. Twelve Dispatches from around the globe show what's happening everywhere else. Every chapter opens with a map.
